# S-Pankki challenge brief (Junction 2026)

**Challenge.** Explore how AI can turn personal banking data into a trusted financial coach for everyday life.
Using a large synthetic transaction dataset modelled on real S-Bank data, build an AI experience that lets
people chat with their own money, from simple questions ("How much did I spend on taxis last month?") to deeper
ones ("What kind of spender am I?", "What is safe for me to spend this week?", "How much could I save if I
reduced dining out by 20 %?").

**Pedagogical angle: three baskets.**
1. Mandatory and regular (e.g. rent)
2. Mandatory and irregular (e.g. a broken washing machine)
3. Everyday spending that slips through your fingers (e.g. coffees)

Solutions that help people understand where their money really goes "will go far". Think MyData: let users
explore their own data freely rather than fixed charts. Open data (e.g. Statistics Finland) for benchmarks is
encouraged, not mandatory.

**Insight.** Interested in conversational AI that explains financial concepts in plain language, simulates
what-if scenarios and guides users to healthier habits, e.g. safe-to-spend and safe-to-save guidance.
Financial literacy among young adults matters especially.

**Optional bonus.** Multi-bank view under PSD2: how could the coach use the customer's full financial picture?

**Output.** A mobile app, or a web app that can be embedded in a mobile app (S-mobiili).

**Context.** S-Bank: 4th largest bank in Finland, 3.5 M customers; S-mobiili used weekly by one in three Finnish
adults. No Finnish bank yet lets customers chat with their own financial data.

**Judging (20 % each).** Impact on financial literacy (incl. pedagogical angle) · Originality of AI integration ·
Clarity of the mobile UX · Feasibility · Potential to integrate into S-mobiili.

**Mentors.** Carl-Edvard (Calle) Holmberg, Chief Innovation Officer (main contact); S-Bank IT/AI developer;
transaction-data data scientist; 1–2 business owners of the customer problem.
